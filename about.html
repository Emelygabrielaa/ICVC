Our story

Built for veterans who chose the Caribbean.

International Caribbean Veterans Care was founded because thousands of U.S. veterans living in the Dominican Republic were flying back to the States every few months for medical care they could be getting here, at the same standard, fully covered by the VA.

Why we exist

An estimated 50,000 U.S. veterans live full-time or part-time in the Dominican Republic. Many retired here for the climate, the cost of living, and the quality of life. But quality healthcare tailored to veterans, billed through the VA, and delivered in English by U.S.- trained physicians, simply didn't exist on this side of the Caribbean.

We built it. International Caribbean Veterans Care is a full-service medical clinic and wellness center in Punta Cana, designed from the ground up for American veterans. Every clinician speaks English. Every service billable to VA's Foreign Medical Program is billed directly, so you don't pay out of pocket and don't chase reimbursements. And every part of the experience, from the waiting room to the retreat program, is built around what veterans actually need.

Our mission

Make world-class healthcare accessible to every U.S. veteran living in, or visiting, the Dominican Republic, at zero cost to them. FMP covers what it covers. For everything else (including dental), the clinic absorbs the cost as part of our commitment to the veterans we serve.

What we do differently

  • We know the FMP system. Our billing team has processed thousands of FMP claims. We know what's covered, what's not, and how to get approvals fast.
  • Bilingual, veteran-focused staff. Every physician and therapist speaks English fluently. Most have U.S. training or U.S. residency credentials.
  • One location, full spectrum. Primary care, specialists, chiropractic, physical therapy, mental health, and diagnostics under one roof.
  • Wellness retreats built in. The Caribbean is a healing environment. We built structured mental health retreat programs that use it.
  • American standards, Caribbean pace. Short waits, direct physician access, and the calm that comes from being on island time.
What guides us

Four commitments we keep.

These aren't marketing copy. They're how we evaluate every clinical decision, every hire, and every patient interaction.

I.

Medicine first, tourism never

We are a medical clinic, not a vacation package. Every treatment is clinically indicated, documented, and delivered to U.S. standards of care.

II.

Zero cost to veterans

U.S. veterans never receive a bill from our clinic. FMP-covered services are billed directly to the VA; non-covered care (including dental) is absorbed by the clinic. No copays, no invoices, no surprises.

III.

Confidentiality, full stop

HIPAA-compliant records. Your medical information stays between you and your provider. We don't share with the VA beyond required billing documentation.

IV.

Veteran-built, veteran-led

Our leadership includes U.S. veterans and medical professionals with deep experience serving veteran populations. We build what we'd want for ourselves.
