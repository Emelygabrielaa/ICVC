How matching works

Your first visit, matched to your needs.

When you book your first appointment, our patient coordinator (Sarah) asks what brought you in: physical concerns, mental health, chronic conditions, or something else. From there, you're matched to the clinician best suited to your situation. You can always request a change if the fit isn't right.
