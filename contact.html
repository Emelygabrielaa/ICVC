What to bring to your first visit

To get you registered with FMP (if needed) and billed correctly, bring the following to your first appointment:

  • Government-issued photo ID (U.S. driver's license, passport, or DR residency)
  • Your DD-214 (discharge paperwork)
  • Your VA rating decision letter, if you have it
  • Your FMP ID number, if already registered
  • A list of current medications
  • Any recent lab results or imaging from U.S. providers (optional but helpful)

Don't have all of this? No problem. Come in anyway, and we'll help you gather what's missing.
