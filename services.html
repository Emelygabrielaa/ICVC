Clinical services

A full-spectrum clinic, one roof.

Primary care, specialists, rehabilitation, mental health, and diagnostics, all delivered by a bilingual medical team and billable to VA's Foreign Medical Program when clinically indicated for service-connected conditions.

Primary & General Medicine Chiropractic & Rehab Mental Health & PTSD Dental Care Urgent & Acute Care Cardiology Internal Medicine Diagnostics & Labs Telehealth
Service 01

Primary & General Medicine

Routine, preventive, and comprehensive primary care for U.S. veterans living in the DR. Whether you need an annual physical, prescription management, a referral, or ongoing care for a chronic condition, our primary care team is your home base.

  • Annual physicals and wellness exams
  • Chronic condition management: hypertension, diabetes, thyroid, COPD, arthritis
  • Prescription management and refills (bilingual prescriptions available)
  • Preventive screenings: colonoscopy referrals, skin checks, labs
  • Vaccinations: flu, shingles, pneumonia, tetanus, travel immunizations
  • Referrals and care coordination with DR specialists when needed

FMP coverage

Primary care visits for service-connected conditions are billed directly to the VA's Foreign Medical Program. Non-service-connected preventive care is provided free of charge by the clinic. Veterans pay nothing, regardless.

Service 02

Chiropractic & Rehabilitation

Back pain, joint issues, and service-connected musculoskeletal injuries are among the most common disability ratings for U.S. veterans. Our chiropractic and rehabilitation program delivers proven, non-surgical protocols for lasting relief.

Our chiropractic and rehab team provides:

  • Chiropractic adjustments for spinal alignment, back pain, and neck pain
  • Physical therapy for post-injury recovery, mobility restoration, and strength training
  • Manual therapy and soft tissue work for chronic pain and muscle tension
  • Rehabilitation programs tailored to service-connected injuries: knees, shoulders, back, hips
  • Pain management coordinated with your VA rating and ongoing care plan

Chiropractic care is explicitly covered by FMP for service-connected musculoskeletal conditions. If you have a rating for back or joint issues, you likely qualify for covered care here.

Service 03

Mental Health & PTSD Care

Trauma-informed therapy for veterans, delivered in a setting built for healing. Our mental health team uses evidence-based protocols with the strongest research backing for veteran populations.

We offer:

  • Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT) for PTSD, the most-researched protocol in veteran populations
  • Prolonged Exposure (PE) for structured, evidence-based trauma processing
  • EMDR for veterans who respond better to non-verbal processing approaches
  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) for depression and anxiety
  • Couples therapy including Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) for military families
  • Group therapy for veterans who benefit from peer connection
  • Moral injury work and Adaptive Disclosure for events that violated deep values

Mental health services for PTSD, depression, anxiety, and other service-connected conditions are fully covered under FMP. Ask about our structured wellness retreats for intensive work.

Service 04

Dental Care

Dental care isn't covered by the VA's Foreign Medical Program for most veterans. But we know untreated dental issues lead to pain, infection, cardiovascular complications, and a long list of quality-of-life problems. So our clinic provides comprehensive dental care free of charge to every U.S. veteran we see.

  • Routine cleanings and preventive care (twice-yearly)
  • Exams, X-rays, and cavity detection
  • Fillings, extractions, and basic restorative work
  • Root canals and crown preparation (partnered with local specialists)
  • Denture fitting, adjustments, and repairs
  • Oral cancer screenings as part of annual exams
  • Emergency dental visits for acute pain or trauma

How we make this work

Because FMP doesn't reimburse dental services, the clinic absorbs these costs as part of our commitment to U.S. veterans in the DR. You walk in, get treated, and walk out. No bill, no paperwork, no catch.

Service 05

Urgent & Acute Care

Illness and injury don't wait for Monday morning. Our urgent care service is built for veterans who need same-day attention without the chaos of an emergency room.

  • Same-day appointments for acute illness, injury, or flare-ups of chronic conditions
  • 24/7 on-call physician access for registered patients (by WhatsApp and phone)
  • Basic procedures: suturing, minor wound care, splinting, injections
  • Rapid diagnostics: EKG, bloodwork, strep, flu, urinalysis
  • Coordination with nearby DR hospitals for emergencies requiring higher-level care

For life-threatening emergencies

Call local emergency services (911 in the DR) or go directly to Hospiten Bavaro or Centro Medico Punta Cana. Our urgent care is for non-life-threatening conditions.

Service 06

Cardiology & Internal Medicine

Cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and related chronic conditions are among the most common issues facing aging veterans. Our in-house specialists provide ongoing care and coordination.

  • Cardiac screening, stress testing, and echocardiograms
  • Hypertension and cholesterol management
  • Diabetes management, including Type 1 and Type 2 support
  • Thyroid disorders and endocrine care
  • Pulmonary function testing and COPD management
  • Coordination with U.S. providers if you maintain care in both countries
Service 07

Diagnostics & Laboratory

Full on-site and partner-lab diagnostic services so you don't have to coordinate care across multiple facilities.

  • On-site lab: CBC, metabolic panels, A1C, lipid panels, thyroid, inflammatory markers, PSA, and most routine panels
  • Imaging referrals: X-ray, ultrasound, CT, MRI through partner facilities with direct billing to FMP when covered
  • EKG and cardiac monitoring in-house
  • Biopsy coordination for pathology review with U.S. or DR partner labs
  • Results in English, reviewed with your physician, sent to your U.S. providers if you request
Service 08

Telehealth & Follow-up Care

For follow-up visits, prescription management, mental health sessions, and non-exam consultations, our telehealth platform makes care accessible from anywhere in the DR.

  • HIPAA-compliant, encrypted video platform
  • Available for primary care follow-ups, mental health sessions, and medication reviews
  • Accessible from any island: Santo Domingo, Santiago, Puerto Plata, Samana, La Romana
  • For veterans temporarily traveling within the region (Puerto Rico, Turks & Caicos, Bahamas)
  • English and Spanish sessions available
