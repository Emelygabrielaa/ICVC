Mental health retreats

Healing in a place built for it.

Structured multi-day wellness retreats for U.S. veterans, combining evidence-based trauma therapy with the restorative environment of the Caribbean coast. Small cohorts, private accommodations, and clinical protocols that actually work.

Why retreats?

Weekly outpatient therapy works for many veterans. For others, especially those managing PTSD, moral injury, or the cumulative weight of multiple deployments, a structured, immersive program produces faster and more durable progress.

Our retreats combine the clinical rigor of intensive trauma protocols (CPT, PE, EMDR) with supportive practices: guided meditation, breathwork, gentle movement, sleep hygiene, and time in nature. Everything is delivered in small cohorts of 6 to 10 veterans, with private lodging, chef-prepared meals, and full concierge support.

FMP and retreat programs

The clinical portion of our retreats (therapy sessions, group work, medical check-ins) is billable to FMP when tied to your service-connected rating. The lodging and wellness activities portion is a separate self-pay component. We outline all costs upfront before you enroll.

Our programs

Three structured retreats, tailored to the work.

Each program is led by a licensed clinician, runs with cohorts of 6 to 10 participants, and includes daily individual therapy, group sessions, wellness activities, and one-on-one check-ins.

RETREAT 01

PTSD Recovery Intensive

7-day structured program combining daily Cognitive Processing Therapy sessions with group work, meditation, and gentle movement. For veterans with diagnosed PTSD or active trauma symptoms.

Cohort size: 6 to 8 veterans
Duration: 7 days / 6 nights
Led by: Dr. Maria Jimenez + David Thompson

RETREAT 02

Couples & Reconnection

5-day retreat for veteran couples. Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) work on reconnection after deployment, combat stress, or long separations. Shared accommodations, private sessions.

Cohort size: 4 to 6 couples
Duration: 5 days / 4 nights
Led by: Patricia Velez, LMFT

RETREAT 03

Transition & Purpose

10-day program for recently separated veterans working through identity, direction, and meaning beyond the uniform. Combines ACT-based therapy, group work, and practical life-design tools.

Cohort size: 8 to 10 veterans
Duration: 10 days / 9 nights
Led by: David Thompson, LCSW

A typical day

Structured, but not rigid.

Retreat days are built around two or three therapeutic anchors (individual therapy, group session, wellness activity) with plenty of space for rest, reflection, beach time, and sleep. Here's what a midweek day might look like on the PTSD Recovery Intensive.

AM

7:00 – Sunrise meditation & breakfast

Optional guided meditation on the beach. Chef-prepared breakfast in the communal dining area.

AM

9:00 – Individual CPT session

90-minute one-on-one session with your assigned therapist. Structured Cognitive Processing Therapy, the most-researched PTSD protocol.

PM

12:00 – Lunch & rest

Shared lunch, then free time. Swim, nap, journal, call family, whatever the day calls for.

PM

3:00 – Group session

Facilitated group processing with the full cohort. Builds peer connection and shared language for the work.

PM

5:00 – Movement & wellness

Gentle yoga, beach walk, or trauma-informed strength training, your choice.

PM

7:00 – Dinner & free evening

Shared dinner, optional campfire on the beach, early bedtime. Sleep is part of the protocol.

Who these retreats are for

Our retreats are designed for veterans who are ready to do focused, structured work, in a supportive environment. You don't need previous therapy experience, but you do need:

  • A willingness to engage in evidence-based trauma protocols (not just "getting away")
  • Medical clearance from your primary care physician
  • Stable housing and support to return to after the retreat
  • No active psychosis, active suicidality, or acute substance withdrawal (we'll help you find the right level of care if that's where you are)

Who these retreats aren't for

If you're in acute crisis, actively suicidal, or currently withdrawing from substances, a retreat isn't the right fit right now. Call the Veterans Crisis Line (988, press 1) or reach out to us directly and we'll help connect you with an appropriate level of care. When you're stabilized, our programs will be here.

What's included

  • All clinical services (individual therapy, group sessions, medical check-ins)
  • Private or shared accommodations (depending on program)
  • Three chef-prepared meals daily, plus snacks
  • All wellness activities (yoga, meditation, movement, beach excursions)
  • Airport pickup and drop-off from Punta Cana International (PUJ)
  • Concierge support for anything you need during your stay
  • Post-retreat integration call at 2 weeks and 6 weeks
