Mental health retreats

Healing in a place built for it.

At ICVC, we believe in a holistic approach to veteran heal thcare, addressing both physical and mental wellbeing. Our Mindfulness & Therapeutic Programs are designed to support veterans in managing stress, PTSD, anxiety, and chronic pain through evidence based techniques.

Our approach

Mindfulness Therapy

Mindfulness is a practice that helps individuals focus on the present moment, promoting relaxation, self-awareness, and emotional regulation.

Our therapeutic services are tailored to each veteran's unique needs, combining clinical rigor with body-based practices for lasting results. Every program is led by licensed professionals who specialize in working with veteran populations.

Mindfulness Practices

  • Guided Meditation reduces stress and improves concentration
  • Breathing Techniques help regulate emotions and decrease anxiety
  • Body Awareness Exercises support pain management and relaxation

Clinical Therapies

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) effective for PTSD, depression, and anxiety
  • Group Therapy connects veterans with shared experiences for mutual support
  • Expressive Therapies art, music, and movement therapy for emotional healing

Physical Rehabilitation

  • Specialized Recovery techniques to rebuild mobility and reduce pain
  • Integrated Care coordinated with your VA rating and treatment plan
  • Whole-Body Approach combining movement with mental health work
