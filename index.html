Why Punta Cana

Healthcare that brings you back to yourself.

Thousands of U.S. veterans live in, or regularly visit, the Dominican Republic. International Caribbean Veterans Care exists to make sure they don't have to fly back to the States for quality healthcare. We deliver American-standard medical and mental health services locally, coordinated with the VA's Foreign Medical Program so you pay little or nothing out of pocket.

Foreign Medical Program

Your VA benefits, across borders.

The VA's Foreign Medical Program (FMP) covers healthcare for service-connected conditions when you're outside the U.S. We're an experienced FMP provider: we handle the paperwork, coordinate with the VA, and most importantly, we never charge U.S. veterans a cent. Whether FMP covers the service or not, your cost is zero.
